1. Information We Collect

At Blosso Artisanal Floristry LLC (“Blosso”, “we”, “our”, or “us”), we respect your privacy. When you browse our online boutique, create a custom arrangement, or place a floral order, we collect only the information necessary to fulfill our bespoke floristry services.

  • Ordering & Contact Details: Your full name, billing address, email address, and telephone number for order verification and transit updates.
  • Recipient Information: Recipient name, doorstep delivery address, apartment/suite numbers, gate codes, and delivery instructions.
  • Calligraphy & Sentiment Messages: Custom messages submitted for hand-lettered cards included with floral arrangements.
  • Technical & Preference Data: IP address, device type, theme preferences (light/dark mode), and text direction (LTR/RTL) for optimized viewing.
Our Promise: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or your recipient’s contact information with external data brokers or advertisers.
💌

2. Recipient Confidentiality & Sentiment Security

🛡️
Private Card Seal

No Spam for Recipients

Recipient phone numbers are strictly used by our chilled fleet drivers for doorstep delivery coordination.

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is utilized strictly to provide our high-touch floristry experience:

  • Processing payments, generating invoices, and confirming your curated stem selection.
  • Dispatching refrigerated delivery vans and providing live SMS/email status notifications.
  • Managing corporate subscription schedules and reminder services for annual milestones.
  • Sending our botanical journal and seasonal masterclass invites when you explicitly join the VIP Circle.
🔒

4. Payment & Encryption Standards

All digital transactions on Blosso are protected by industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption. We adhere strictly to PCI-DSS compliance protocols.

Zero Card Storage: Your credit card, debit card, or digital wallet details are processed directly through tokenized payment gateways. We never store raw credit card numbers on our local servers.
🍪

5. Cookies & Interface Preferences

We use strictly essential local storage and lightweight cookies to preserve your cart items, remember your selected dark/light theme, and maintain your RTL/LTR language preference across page navigations.

⚖️

6. Your Rights (CCPA & GDPR Compliance)

Under California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) frameworks, you hold the following rights:

  • Right of Access: Request a complete record of your stored personal details and past order history.
  • Right to Deletion: Request permanent removal of your account, addresses, and transaction metadata from our active systems.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing dispatches at any time with a single click in any newsletter footer.
🏛️

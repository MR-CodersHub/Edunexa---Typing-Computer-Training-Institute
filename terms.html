2. Artisanal Nature & Botanical Substitution Policy

Flowers are living, seasonal creations of nature. While our florists meticulously match the style, palette, and botanical prestige displayed in our catalog, micro-variations in blossom size, hue saturation, and branch curvature naturally occur.

Luxury Substitution Guarantee: If a specific seasonal stem (e.g. Juliet Garden Rose, King Protea, or French Ranunculus) is unavailable due to farm-harvest climate conditions, our master florists will substitute it with a stem of equal or greater luxury tier and identical tonal palette.

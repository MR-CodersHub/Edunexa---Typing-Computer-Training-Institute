Master Florist Secrets

4 Steps to Double Your Bouquet’s Vase Life

Follow these simple steps when your Blosso delivery arrives at your door.

1

Diagonal 45° Stem Cut

Trim 1 inch off the bottom of each stem at a 45-degree angle underwater to prevent air bubbles from blocking hydration.

2

Clean Vessel & Food

Wash your glass vase thoroughly with mild soap and fill with lukewarm filtered water mixed with our organic nutrient packet.

3

Strip Submerged Foliage

Remove any leaves that will sit below the waterline. Submerged foliage creates bacterial growth that prematurely wilts stems.

4

Shield from Direct Heat

Place blooms in bright, indirect light away from radiators, air conditioners, and fruit bowls (ethylene gas accelerates aging).

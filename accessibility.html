1. Conformance Standard (WCAG 2.1 Level AA)

Blosso is dedicated to providing an accessible online experience for all visitors. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA benchmarks and Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) title standards.

These guidelines define how to make web content more accessible to individuals with visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor disabilities.
✨

2. Accessible Features Built into Blosso

We have incorporated modern assistive tools directly into the Blosso interface:

🌗
Dark & High-Contrast Mode

Instant toggle reduces eye strain and provides optimal text-to-background contrast ratios exceeding 4.5:1.

🔄
Bidirectional RTL / LTR

Native right-to-left layout switch supporting Arabic and Hebrew readers with full keyboard parity.

⌨️
Keyboard Navigability

Logical tab sequences, visible focus rings, and skip links across all filters and modal overlays.

🏷️
Descriptive ARIA Labels

Comprehensive screen reader announcements for buttons, dialog states, and stem quantity adjusters.

🎧

3. Assistive Technology Compatibility

Our digital shop is engineered to be compatible with major assistive technologies and modern web browsers:

  • Screen Readers: Tested with NVDA (Windows), JAWS, Apple VoiceOver (macOS & iOS), and Android TalkBack.
  • Magnification & Zoom: Seamless interface scaling up to 200% without loss of content structure or functionality.
  • Color Perception: Content does not rely solely on color to convey vital information like stock availability or delivery dates.
🏛️

4. Physical Flagship Boutique Accommodations

When visiting our Manhattan Flagship Boutique at 742 Evergreen Botanical Way:

  • Step-Free Entry: Level ground entrance and automated double glass entry doors.
  • Wide Floral Walkways: Spacious 5-foot clearance between display tables accommodating motorized wheelchairs and strollers.
  • Complimentary Porte-Cochère Valet: Accessible curbside vehicle drop-off and assisted entry.
  • Service Animals: Certified service animals are welcome throughout all retail and conservatory areas.

3. Climate-Shielded Packaging & Hydration Reservoirs

Unlike standard postal deliveries that cram flowers into cardboard cartons, Blosso uses an architectural unboxing experience:

  • Aqua-Gel Stem Wraps: Every cut stem base is sealed in nutrient-infused botanical water packs providing 48 hours of continuous hydration.
  • Rigid Fluted Travel Carriers: Upright bespoke presentation boxes preventing any petal crushing during transport.
  • Thermal Insulative Sleeves: Protects delicate blooms against extreme winter freezing or summer heat waves.
🏡

4. Recipient Unavailability & Safe Drop Protocol

If the recipient is unavailable upon doorstep arrival:

  • Our courier will first contact the recipient via discreet phone call/SMS.
  • If permitted, the arrangement will be placed in a climate-sheltered porch area or with building concierge/doorman with photo confirmation sent immediately.
  • If temperature conditions are extreme and no safe drop exists, the bouquet returns to our chilled floral studio and complimentary re-delivery is coordinated.
🏥

5. Hospitals, Hotels & Corporate Venues

For deliveries to medical centers or luxury hotels, our white-glove team delivers directly to the designated receiving desk or guest concierge with full recipient room/suite documentation to ensure timely internal transfer.

🌿

6. 7-Day Botanical Freshness Guarantee

We stand wholeheartedly behind every bloom. If your bouquet does not remain vibrant and radiant for at least 7 full days with proper water replenishment, simply send a photo to our concierge team at concierge@blosso.com for an immediate replacement bouquet or full store credit.
